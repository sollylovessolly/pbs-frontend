## Privacy policy — approved customer wording

**Your photos belong to you.** Uploading a photo to PolaroidsBySolly does not give us permission to post it. We use your photos and personalised messages to prepare your keepsakes and help with your order.

**We will not post your photos or personalised letters on social media, our website, advertising or our portfolio without your separate, explicit permission.** Choosing not to give that permission will not affect your order. If you agree to a particular use and later change your mind, contact us to withdraw permission for future use and request removal from channels we control. We cannot guarantee removal of copies already shared by others.

### Information we use

When you shop with us, we may receive your name, email address, phone number, delivery address, uploaded photos, letter text, order details and payment references/status. Our website also saves your cart in your browser and keeps checkout recovery information for your current browser session.

We use the information needed to make and deliver your order, provide updates, resolve problems and maintain necessary business records. Preparing your order is separate from any optional permission to feature it publicly.

### Payments and service providers

Paystack handles payment processing. Our checkout receives payment references and payment status; it does not ask you to send card details to the studio.

Our hosting, file-storage and other operational providers may process information needed to run the shop. Delivery providers receive the contact and delivery details needed to deliver your parcel. Any production partner should receive only the material needed to make your keepsake and must be required to keep it confidential. We do not sell your photos or personal information.

## Terms of order — approved customer wording

### Placing your order

Please review the selected product, phone model, quantity, crop, letter text and delivery details before paying. Your uploaded design is used to prepare your personalised item. Contact us quickly if you spot a mistake so we can check whether a change is still possible.

Prices are shown in Nigerian naira. Delivery fees and any accepted discount are confirmed during checkout. Payment must be confirmed before production begins. If your payment status is uncertain, use “Check payment status” or contact us before paying again.

### Personalised products

You keep ownership of your photos and text. You give us permission to reproduce them only as needed to make your order. This does not give us permission to advertise with them or post them publicly.

Clear original photos give the best results. Colours may vary slightly between screens and printed materials. This does not remove your rights if an item is faulty, materially different from the order or incorrectly produced.

### Preparation and delivery

The preparation time shown on each product is separate from delivery time. Delivery depends on your location and the delivery service. We will let you know about significant delays we become aware of. Please provide a reachable phone number and complete address.

### Changes, cancellations and problems

Polaroids, vintage letters and other customised items are made specifically for you. Once processing of your personalised order has started, we do not offer refunds for a change of mind or a cancellation request, except where required by law. Please check your photos, crop, wording and selected options carefully before placing your order.

Contact us as soon as possible to request a change or cancellation before processing begins. Once processing has started, changes may no longer be possible. We will explain the available options and any costs before agreeing to them.

If an item arrives damaged, faulty or different from your order, contact us with your order number and details of the problem. We will investigate and arrange an appropriate remedy, such as a replacement or refund, in line with your applicable consumer rights. These terms do not remove rights provided by law.
