Use actual JPG files, ideally portrait (1200 × 1500 px), under 500 KB each. Leave some space around the product; different cards crop the picture differently. Bright natural light and a simple background work well. Avoid added prices or promotional text in the picture.

These are display photos only. Do not replace frame artwork, strip templates or letter templates to update product pictures. The customiser previews and production files are separate.
